News that is easier to see, hear, and follow.

Klarblatt is a clear, uncluttered reader for people who are blind, losing their sight, or simply want reading to take less effort. Follow topics, see the main points first, and listen to any article.

Easy reading, from the first page

Choose topics, not feed addresses

Follow subjects such as blindness, eye health, clinical research, science, books, or world news. Klarblatt finds the sources.

Large type and strong contrast

Choose your text size, colours, spacing, and font. Bigger and smaller text buttons stay within reach on every page.

Main points first

Headings, bullet points, and important passages appear before the full article, using the publisher's own words.

Listen to articles

Read-aloud uses your browser's speech voices and highlights each paragraph as it is spoken.

Save what matters

Keep articles in a saved list that is separate from whether you have already read them.

Your account, your reading

Your topics, articles, and display choices are private to your account. Send-to-Kindle stays optional.

Three steps to start reading

  1. 1

    Create your account

    Sign up with an e-mail and password, then confirm your address.

  2. 2

    Choose your topics

    Pick the subjects you care about. You do not need to know what RSS is.

  3. 3

    Make it comfortable

    Set the type, colours, and spacing, then read or listen.
